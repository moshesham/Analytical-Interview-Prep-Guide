# Product Analytics Case Study Framework

## Introduction
Product case studies are a cornerstone of analytics interviews at top companies. They test your ability to think structurally, apply analytical frameworks, use data to inform decisions, and communicate insights clearly.

This document provides a battle-tested framework for tackling product case studies, whether they're about investigating metric changes, evaluating new features, or diagnosing product issues.

**Common Case Study Types:**
1. **Root Cause Analysis:** "DAU dropped 5% last week. Investigate."
2. **Feature Evaluation:** "Should we add [feature]? How would you decide?"
3. **Metric Investigation:** "Engagement is down but revenue is up. What's happening?"
4. **Product Improvement:** "How would you improve [product]?"
5. **Trade-off Analysis:** "Feature A vs. Feature B—which should we build?"

## Framework Overview
1. **Understanding the Problem**
   - Clarify the question being asked.
   - Identify key objectives and constraints.
   - Ask clarifying questions to gather more context.

2. **Structuring Your Approach**
   - Break down the problem into manageable components.
   - Use frameworks such as SWOT, Porter’s Five Forces, or the 4Ps (Product, Price, Place, Promotion) as applicable.
   - Create a logical flow for your analysis.

3. **Data Analysis**
   - Identify the data needed to support your analysis.
   - Discuss potential sources of data and how to obtain them.
   - Analyze the data to draw insights and conclusions.

4. **Generating Solutions**
   - Brainstorm potential solutions or recommendations.
   - Evaluate the pros and cons of each solution.
   - Consider the feasibility and impact of your recommendations.

5. **Communicating Your Findings**
   - Summarize your analysis and recommendations clearly.
   - Use visual aids (charts, graphs) if applicable to enhance understanding.
   - Prepare to answer follow-up questions and defend your conclusions.

## Example Case Study Question
**Question:** User engagement on Instagram Reels has dropped by 5%. How would you investigate?

### Step-by-Step Approach
1. **Understanding the Problem**
   - What specific metrics indicate a drop in engagement?
   - Are there particular demographics or user segments affected?

2. **Structuring Your Approach**
   - Analyze user behavior before and after the drop.
   - Consider external factors (e.g., competition, market trends).

3. **Data Analysis**
   - Gather data on user interactions, content types, and posting frequency.
   - Compare engagement metrics across different time periods.

4. **Generating Solutions**
   - Identify potential reasons for the drop (e.g., content quality, algorithm changes).
   - Propose strategies to improve engagement (e.g., new content formats, targeted campaigns).

5. **Communicating Your Findings**
   - Present your analysis in a clear, structured manner.
   - Use data visualizations to support your recommendations.

## Interview Tips and Best Practices

### Do's ✅
- **Think out loud:** Share your thought process, not just conclusions
- **Ask clarifying questions:** Shows you don't jump to conclusions
- **Be structured:** Use frameworks to organize your thinking
- **Use data:** Mention specific metrics and how you'd measure them
- **Consider trade-offs:** Acknowledge pros and cons of recommendations
- **Be hypothesis-driven:** Form and test hypotheses systematically

### Don'ts ❌
- **Don't stay silent:** Interviewers can't evaluate what they can't hear
- **Don't ramble:** Stay structured and focused
- **Don't ignore follow-ups:** Listen and adapt to interviewer questions
- **Don't be too theoretical:** Ground answers in practical examples
- **Don't forget the business:** Always connect back to business impact
- **Don't claim certainty:** Acknowledge what you'd need to verify

### Time Management
- **Clarify:** 2-3 minutes (20%)
- **Structure:** 2-3 minutes (20%)
- **Analyze:** 5-7 minutes (40%)
- **Recommend:** 2-3 minutes (20%)

## Common Case Study Patterns

### Pattern 1: Metric Drop Investigation
**Examples:** "DAU dropped 5%", "Conversion rate decreased"
**Framework:** Internal vs. External factors, then segment analysis
**Key Questions:** When? Which segments? What changed?

### Pattern 2: Feature Prioritization
**Examples:** "Should we build X or Y?", "How would you prioritize?"
**Framework:** User problem + Business impact + Feasibility
**Key Questions:** Who benefits? How much impact? What's the cost?

### Pattern 3: Product Improvement
**Examples:** "How would you improve Spotify?", "Fix low retention"
**Framework:** Identify problems → Prioritize → Propose solutions
**Key Questions:** Biggest pain points? Quick wins vs. long-term?

## Conclusion

Mastering the case study framework requires practice. The 5-step approach—Clarify, Structure, Analyze, Synthesize, Recommend—provides a reliable foundation for any product analytics case study.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Always start by clarifying the problem and constraints
- Use structured frameworks to organize your thinking
- Be hypothesis-driven in your analysis
- Support recommendations with data and logic
- Practice with real interview questions regularly
