## Refine: Targeted Practice Sessions

### Session 1: Priority 1 Gap (60-90 minutes)

**Example: "I struggled with SQL window functions"**

**Targeted Practice Plan:**
1. **Review (20 min):** Re-read Week 1 advanced SQL materials, focus on window functions
2. **Guided Practice (30 min):** Work through 3-5 example problems with solutions available
3. **Independent Practice (30 min):** Solve 2-3 similar problems without looking at solutions
4. **Reflection (10 min):** What clicked? What's still confusing?

**Resources by Common Gaps:**

**SQL Window Functions:**
- Mode Analytics SQL tutorial (window functions section)
- LeetCode SQL problems tagged "Window Function"
- Practice: Running totals, moving averages, rank/dense_rank patterns

**Python Pandas:**
- Kaggle "Pandas" micro-course (free)
- Practice: groupby, merge, pivot_table, datetime operations
- Work through 2-3 Kaggle datasets with guided analysis

**Product Frameworks:**
- Re-watch product case study walkthroughs on YouTube (Exponent, IGotAnOffer)
- Practice applying CIRCLES to 3 new product questions
- Record yourself and evaluate structure and clarity

**Behavioral Storytelling:**
- Rewrite your weakest STAR story with more specificity and impact
- Practice out loud 5 times, timing yourself (2-3 min target)
- Focus on the "A" (Action) and "R" (Result) components

### Session 2: Priority 2 Gap (45-60 minutes)

Follow the same structure:
1. Review concept/framework (15 min)
2. Guided practice (20 min)
3. Independent application (20 min)
4. Reflection (5 min)

### Session 3: Mock Interview Redo (30-45 minutes)

**Concept:** Retry a problem you struggled with initially

**Process:**
1. Set timer for realistic interview duration
2. Attempt the problem again without looking at notes
3. Compare your new approach to your original attempt
4. Identify improvements and remaining gaps

**Example:**
- **Original Performance:** Got stuck on window function logic, took 35 min for a 25 min problem
- **Retry Performance:** Completed in 20 min with correct approach and testing
- **Lesson:** I understand the concept now, just need to practice speed

## Rest: Recovery & Mental Preparation

### Physical Recovery (Essential)

**Sleep:**
- Aim for 8+ hours tonight
- Go to bed at your normal time (don't disrupt schedule before interviews)
- Avoid screens 1 hour before bed

**Nutrition:**
- Eat regular, balanced meals
- Stay hydrated throughout the day
- Avoid excessive caffeine (maintain normal intake, don't spike)

**Physical Activity:**
- Light exercise: 30-minute walk, yoga, stretching
- Get outside if possible (nature reduces stress)
- Avoid intense workouts that might leave you sore

### Mental Recovery (Critical)

**Active Rest Activities:**

**Option 1: Nature & Mindfulness (Highly Recommended)**
- 30-60 minute walk in a park or natural setting
- Practice mindful observation: notice sights, sounds, sensations
- Leave phone at home or in airplane mode

**Option 2: Creative Outlet**
- Engage in a hobby unrelated to work/interviews
- Music, art, cooking, reading fiction
- Allows your brain to process learning subconsciously

**Option 3: Social Connection**
- Spend quality time with friends or family
- Talk about non-interview topics
- Laughter and connection reduce stress hormones

**Option 4: Gentle Mental Stimulation**
- Watch an educational documentary (not about data or tech!)
- Play a strategy game or puzzle
- Read about a topic you're curious about

**What to Avoid:**
- ❌ Binge-studying late into the night
- ❌ Dwelling on mistakes or perceived weaknesses
- ❌ Comparing yourself to others
- ❌ Social media rabbit holes
- ❌ Stressful news or content
- ❌ All-or-nothing thinking ("If I don't master X, I'll fail")

### Mindfulness & Stress Management Techniques

**Technique 1: Box Breathing (5 minutes, repeat as needed)**
1. Inhale for 4 counts
2. Hold for 4 counts
3. Exhale for 4 counts
4. Hold for 4 counts
5. Repeat for 5-10 cycles

**When to use:** When feeling anxious about upcoming interviews

**Technique 2: Positive Visualization (10 minutes)**
1. Find a quiet, comfortable space
2. Close your eyes and visualize yourself in the interview
3. See yourself answering confidently, thinking clearly
4. Visualize the interviewer smiling, nodding
5. Feel the sense of accomplishment and relief after a successful interview

**When to use:** Before bed, or when confidence is low

**Technique 3: Gratitude & Perspective (5 minutes)**
1. Write down 3 things you're grateful for about your preparation
2. Acknowledge how far you've come since starting Week 1
3. Remember: You're prepared, and one interview doesn't define you

**When to use:** When feeling overwhelmed or discouraged

## Key Takeaways

1. **Review systematically** - Use structured frameworks to assess performance
2. **Prioritize ruthlessly** - Focus on top 2-3 gaps, not everything
3. **Practice deliberately** - Targeted, focused practice on specific weaknesses
4. **Acknowledge strengths** - Confidence comes from knowing what you do well
5. **Rest is essential** - Your brain consolidates learning during rest
6. **Mental preparation matters** - Mindset is as important as skills
7. **Balance is key** - Don't burn out right before interviews

**Remember:** You've done the work. Week 3 is about fine-tuning and preparing mentally. Trust your preparation, identify and address gaps, then rest. You're almost ready!
